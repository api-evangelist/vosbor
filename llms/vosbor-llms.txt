# Vosbor

> Vosbor is a digital trading platform for bulk agricultural commodities — corn, wheat, soybean, palm oil and other grains and oilseeds — offering spot and forward markets, integrated KYC, encrypted trade data, straight-through processing, and a WhatsApp AI broker. Headquartered in Amsterdam (Vosbor Exchange B.V.) with offices in Singapore and Shanghai; founded in 2019 and backed by Lux Capital.
